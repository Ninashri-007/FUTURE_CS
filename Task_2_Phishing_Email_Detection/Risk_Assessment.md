## Risk Evaluation Criteria

### High Risk

Characteristics:

* Spoofed sender domains
* Suspicious links
* Credential harvesting attempts
* Urgent language
* Threats of suspension or account closure

### Medium Risk

Characteristics:

* Unknown sender
* Unverified attachments
* Suspicious formatting

### Low Risk

Characteristics:

* Verified sender domain
* No suspicious links
* Professional communication
* No urgency or threats

## Overall Assessment

Three of the four analyzed emails contain strong phishing indicators and pose a significant risk to users. One email was classified as legitimate based on sender authenticity, content quality, and absence of malicious indicators.
